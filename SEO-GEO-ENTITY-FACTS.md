## What EdBuddies is

A centre-management platform/app built for education businesses. Brings
daily centre operations into one place, reducing reliance on separate
spreadsheets, chat groups and paper records.

## Who it serves

- Tuition centres
- Enrichment centres
- Schools
- Freelance tutors
- Both smaller operators and multi-centre businesses

## What EdBuddies is NOT

- Not a student-learning app
- Not a tutor marketplace
- Not only an AI education platform

## Markets

- **Active/approved for marketing:** Malaysia and Singapore
- **Not approved:** Vietnam, the Philippines — must not be promoted or
  described as active markets until Kenneth confirms launch readiness
- Singapore is treated as the primary market for current SEO/GEO work
  (session instruction), without contradicting the "both markets" framing
  above — order/emphasis, not exclusivity

## Platforms

iOS and Android.

## Approved core capabilities

- Students and parents
- Teachers

- Attendance
- Billing and invoicing
- Payment tracking
- Parent and teacher communication
- Homework
- Finance and daily administration
- Teacher leave
- Payroll

## Pricing — hard rule

EdBuddies is a paid product, but pricing is confidential in all public
marketing. Never mention, imply, or let AI-search summarization infer:

- A price or cost figure
- "Free" or "free forever" (as an unqualified claim)
- Subscription amounts
- Student-commission comparisons
- Promotional price periods, unless Kenneth approves the exact campaign

Pricing is explained privately, after a prospect contacts EdBuddies.
